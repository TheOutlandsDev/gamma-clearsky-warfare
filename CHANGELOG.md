# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-22

Initial source testing release: original optional solo addons, sanitized experimental host/join launcher and bridge, setup/preflight tooling, campaign reference presets, component permission ledger and public feedback forms. Third-party games/mods/engines are dependencies rather than bundled downloads.

Two-PC gameplay, public-install compatibility, full solo-to-co-op parity and long-campaign balance remain unverified. AlifeTactics is a proposed separate solo trial only.
